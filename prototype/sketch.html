Design principles

Compact navigation, generous space around the main task, neutral surfaces, subtle borders, and colour reserved for status. The prototype includes light and dark themes. Explanations reveal more detail as needed; they do not obscure the account overview.

Prototype boundary: synthetic aggregates and client-side rules. Python ingestion, SQL, schema-shaped 90-day data and Model-assisted narrative generation remain future implementation work.
